Our story

Made in Penang, sent across Malaysia

Oh! Gift began with a simple frustration: corporate gifting in Malaysia too often meant the same tired hamper, wrapped in a hurry, arriving late. We thought the moment deserved better.

From our studio in Penang we curate every box by hand — sourcing from makers we have actually met, choosing packaging that feels considered, and checking each gift before it leaves us. The result is something a client is genuinely pleased to open, and a company is proud to have sent.

Whether it is thirty boxes for your team or three thousand for a nationwide campaign, the standard does not change.
